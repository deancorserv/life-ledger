How each pillar is scored

Physical. Bench and squat are converted to bodyweight multiples, then multiplied by a masters age coefficient so an older lifter is compared against what is realistic at that age. The 5km is age graded the same way and then scored against distance running benchmarks. Stop training and both lifts go to zero, whatever you used to do. No recent 5km and the run goes to zero. Split inside the pillar: squat 35, run 35, bench 30.

Financial. Salary is not collected anywhere. You enter what is actually left at the end of the month, and everything in the table above is charged back against it. That adjusted figure is compared to what someone your age should be clearing. Because there is no income to divide by, discipline is measured as banked against burn, meaning how many months of living costs you put away for every month you live. Runway is liquid savings divided by your true monthly cost. Net worth is expressed as years of your own life you own outright, against a target that rises about a quarter of a year for every year you have been an adult. Bad debt subtracts up to 25 points, measured in months of your cost of living.

Independence. Housing, food, transport, accounts in your own name, whether your phone and laptop are paid for outright, and whether you could absorb a real emergency without phoning family. A contract counts for less than cash, and a bigger contract counts for less than a small one. This is deliberately double counted with the financial pillar. The charge back makes the money comparison fair, this pillar scores the state itself.

Education. Your highest South African NQF level sets the base, from NQF 1 at Grade 9 through NQF 4 for matric to NQF 10 for a doctorate. From NQF 5 upward that base is multiplied by a field weighting. Engineering, medicine, accounting, law and actuarial science sit at 1.00. Computer science, pharmacy, architecture and the natural sciences follow just behind. Business, teaching, psychology and marketing sit lower, and humanities and arts sit lowest. An artisan trade qualification is rated at 0.85, above general business, because in this country it is worth more than the paper suggests. Below NQF 5 the field is ignored and only the level counts. A qualification still in progress scores at 70 percent.

Spiritual. Practice consistency, giving and service, vice load (alcohol, smoking, gambling), relationship status, and three self reported readings on purpose, stress and close relationships. The self reported part is the only soft input in the whole system, which is why it carries a small share.

Gaming. Fortnite solo win rate, Call of Duty kill/death and Rainbow Six Siege kill/death, each on its own curve because a 1.5 in Siege is not a 1.5 in Call of Duty. Only the games you tick are scored and the pillar is the average across them, so playing one game well is not punished. Tick nothing and the pillar is zero. This carries the smallest default weight of the six, and it is the easiest number in the system to check on someone's tracker.

Receipts. Verified numbers score at full value. Partly evidenced loses 5 percent of the total. Pure claims lose 12 percent. Enough to decide a close one.
